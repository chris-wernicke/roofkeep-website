5. Online mode (multiplayer)

Only when you start online mode does your browser connect to our game server. We then process:

  • IP address – technically required for the connection; kept in memory only while connected, never stored.
  • Player name – freely chosen (please do not use your real name); on game platforms your username there is suggested so friends can recognise you. It is shown to players in your room and kept only while you are in the room. Names containing links are replaced automatically.
  • Room code and game actions – shared with your room in real time and discarded after the match.
  • Anonymous operating statistics – number of concurrent players and rooms and server load, without IP addresses, names or other identifiers.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the online game you chose) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (server security and stability). Participation is voluntary; online play is not possible without this data.

6. Anonymous play-flow counter

So that we can tell whether the game loads well and where players stop, the game reports to our server which stages were reached during a visit (e.g. “game loaded”, “round started”, “round cleared”, “played for 3 minutes”, “left in the menu”, time of day in 3-hour blocks), together with the game version, the game portal and whether it runs on a touch device. Only totals per day, game version, portal and device type are stored. There is no identifier, no cookie and nothing is stored on your device; individual players or devices cannot be told apart. Your IP address is transmitted for technical reasons, counted only briefly in memory to prevent abuse (requests per minute) and neither stored nor logged. The totals are deleted after 30 days.

If “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” is switched on in your browser, the game sends nothing.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the game and detecting loading problems). You can object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR), e.g. via the browser settings mentioned.

7. Leaderboard (daily challenge)

Only if you tap “Submit” yourself after a daily challenge do we send the following to our game server:

  • Player name – freely chosen (please do not use your real name); it is visible to all players on the leaderboard. Offensive names and names containing links are replaced automatically.
  • Result – wave reached and number of drones destroyed.
  • Random device ID – a randomly generated string from your save data so that only your best result counts. It does not identify you and is not shown to others.

Your IP address is only counted briefly in memory to prevent abuse (requests per minute) and is not stored. Entries are deleted automatically after 7 days.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the leaderboard you chose) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protection against abuse). Submitting is voluntary; you can play the challenge just the same without it.
